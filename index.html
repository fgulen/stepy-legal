b) Çocuk profili

  • Çocuğun adı (veli girer), avatar seçimi, yaş grubu (küçük/büyük) ve dil tercihi.

  • Puan (XP), seviye, haftalık düzen ve bunlardan türetilen istatistikler.

c) Fotoğraflar (görev kanıtı)

  • Çocuğun çektiği görev fotoğrafı yapay zekâ ile analiz edilir. Sonuç olarak tamamlandı / kısmen / tamamlanmadı durumu ve kısa bir geri bildirim üretilir.

  • Uygulamanın yerel veritabanı ve bildirim zamanlamaları cihazda tutulur.

Stepy is a task and focus app for children aged 7–14 who find it hard to concentrate, complete homework and stay motivated. Only a parent or legal guardian creates the account. The child uses the App on their own phone, linked to the parent's account. This policy covers data about both.

2. What data we process

a) Parent account: e-mail address and password. The password is stored encrypted by our authentication provider; Stepy cannot read it. Consent record (consent text version and time). Server access logs including IP address, kept by the infrastructure provider.

b) Child profile: first name (entered by the parent), avatar, age group (junior/senior), language. Tasks: title, category (homework / study / habit), duration and status. Sessions: start and end times, completion. Points (XP), level, weekly rhythm and statistics derived from these.

c) Photos (task proof): the task photo the child takes is analysed by an AI model. The result is a status (complete / partial / not complete) and a short encouraging message. The parent sees only the cropped relevant part. Before anything is sent, the phone checks the photo for faces on the device. If a face is found, the photo is not sent at all and the child is asked to photograph only the book page. The check uses Google ML Kit and is not perfect. The full photo is kept for no more than 7 days and then deleted. If the parent completes the review earlier, the full photo may be deleted earlier. The result text and the crop may remain.

d) Device data: the local database and scheduled reminders are kept on the device.

No analytics SDKs, advertising IDs or third-party tracking are used.

Diagnosis and health information: Stepy does not ask for, and does not store, a child's diagnosis, medical record or health history. The information entered relates only to tasks and study habits. [Counsel to confirm that this does not fall under GDPR Art. 9 / KVKK m.6.]
